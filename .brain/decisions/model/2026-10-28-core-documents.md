# Core document kinds

## Decision

The core of a brain is the index (`README.md`) and decisions (`decisions/`). The roadmap, risks, areas and steps are optional. A step has a common structure: goal, scope, out of scope, decisions, acceptance criteria, stages, log, open questions ([core-model.md](../../architecture/core-model.md)).
