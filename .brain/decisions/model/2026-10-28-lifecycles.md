# Lifecycles of a decision and a step

## Decision

Decision statuses as in ADR: `proposed` (to be decided), `accepted` (made), `rejected` (rejected), `superseded` (superseded, with a reference to the new one). Step statuses: `draft`, `agreed`, `in-progress`, `done`. The values are English keys in the frontmatter ([core-model.md](../../architecture/core-model.md)).
