# The status of a step visible in the content

## Context

A git host (e.g. GitHub) shows the frontmatter as a table above the document, but an editor, a terminal and many markdown previews hide it or show it as raw YAML, and a person must see the status of a step. The status matters for the work: a person sets `agreed`, and work starts only on an `agreed` step ([decision](../collaboration/2026-11-03-plan-before-work.md)).

## Decision

The source of the status and `assignee` of a step is the frontmatter, and a person sees them in two generated views:

- **The status line in the step.** Directly under the title of the step there is a block `<!-- BEGIN:dotbrain status -->` … `<!-- END:dotbrain status -->` with one line: the label of the `status` key, the status value in code, and when the step has `assignee`, the separator ` · `, the label of the `assignee` key and the identities separated by commas (e.g. "Status: `in-progress` · Assignee: user:dkowcun"). The labels come from the `sections` map in the manifest, like the group headings of the decision list. The block is mandatory in every step.
- **The status in the index block.** The line of a step in the index block of `README.md` ends with the status in code in parentheses (e.g. "… (`done`)").

Both views are generated by `index`, `V-VIEWS` reports them when they are stale or missing, and a conflict is resolved by regeneration. This is an exception to the rule "the status is not repeated in the content" of the [required minimal frontmatter](2026-10-28-document-frontmatter.md): by hand the status is changed only in the frontmatter.

## Consequences

- The views are described in `spec/documents.md` (the `status` and `assignee` keys in the `sections` map), `spec/generated-views.md` and `spec/validity.md` (`V-VIEWS`; a view built from a step that breaks `V-STEP` is not compared). The labels are in the templates of the step and the index.
- Rule 7 of the agent contract and its concise form say: after changing the `status` or `assignee` of a step, its status line and the index block are generated again.

## Who decides and when

The owner of the brain (dkowcun).
