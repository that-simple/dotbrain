# License

## Context

The project is to be open; the license affects adoption in companies and outside contributions. The publication of the npm package `@that-simple/dotbrain` and of the repository template waits for this decision ([distribution channels](2026-10-28-distribution-channels.md)).

The framework is not only code. The templates from `templates/` end up in users' repositories: `init` builds from them the skeleton of the brain, the managed blocks in instruction files, the starter decisions and the review subagent, and without the tool the same files are copied by a person or an agent ([setup.md](../../architecture/setup.md)). MIT and Apache-2.0 require keeping the license notice when copying a substantial part of the work, and Apache-2.0 also marking changed files.

Patents matter little in this project: it is a file format, a specification and a small CLI without dependencies.

## Options

- MIT for the whole repository: the simplest and most popular; the dotbrain notice formally in every brain set up from the templates.
- Apache-2.0 for the whole repository: an explicit patent license, more often required by large organizations; the notice and marking of changes also in templates copied into other people's repositories.
- MIT for the code and the specification, MIT-0 for `templates/`: the output of `init` and of setting up by hand carries no obligations.
- Apache-2.0 for the code and the specification, MIT-0 for `templates/`: as above, with a patent license for the code.

## Decision

The whole repository, together with the specification and the templates, is under the MIT license. This is the simplest thing that works ([decision](../model/2026-10-28-simplest-thing-that-works.md)): one license, while Apache-2.0 in a project without a real patent risk adds obligations without benefit. The obligation of the notice for templates copied into other people's repositories is in practice a formality, because the rights belong to the owner of the project, and the output of `init` is to a large extent the user's content.

## Consequences

When someone (e.g. the legal department of a pilot) needs the templates without the obligation of the notice, `templates/` can additionally be made available under MIT-0; this is a relaxation that changes nothing for existing users. The `LICENSE` file in the root directory and the `license` field in the npm package come together with the work on the package.

## Who decides and when

The owner of the project, before the first publication.
