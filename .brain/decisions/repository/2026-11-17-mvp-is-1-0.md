# The MVP is version 1.0, without phases after it

## Context

The roadmap ([roadmap.md](../../roadmap.md)) had Phase 1 (`new`, `export`) and Phase 2 (plugins and skills, public documentation) after the MVP. Neither had a gate or a step, and decisions referred things postponed for later to Phase 2. The work that actually comes after the MVP, that is the publication ([step 4](../../steps/04-npm-package.md), [step 5](../../steps/05-brain-in-english.md)), was in no phase.

## Decision

The scope of the MVP is version 1.0 of dotbrain. The first publication releases the npm package in version `1.0.0`. The roadmap has no phases after the MVP. `new`, `export`, the context package for chats and plugins and skills are outside 1.0, without a plan or a date. They come back with a step or a decision when someone needs them.

## Consequences

The descriptions in the architecture ([tooling-and-distribution.md](../../architecture/tooling-and-distribution.md), [ai-integrations.md](../../architecture/ai-integrations.md)) stay as a description of possibilities outside 1.0. References to Phase 2 in decisions and the architecture change to "outside 1.0". Step 4 releases the package as `1.0.0`. This decision does not change the version of the specification (`spec: 0.1`).
