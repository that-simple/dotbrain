# Steps named by ticket keys

## Decision

A step is named by the key of its ticket in the issue tracker in lowercase instead of a number: `steps/rp-111-short-name.md` for the ticket `RP-111`. The ticket key is the ID of the step and is never renumbered; the order of steps is kept in the tracker or the roadmap. Steps written before this decision keep their numbers.
